# NAICS guidance — couriers versus storage

The illustrative code 492110 relates to Couriers and Express Delivery Services. A warehouse storing goods for weeks does not become a courier solely because packages are handled. Distinguish delivery fleets, parcel sorting hubs, inventory warehouses and fulfillment centers by their principal activities. The examples are not official NAICS determinations.
