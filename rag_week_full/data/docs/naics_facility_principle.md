# NAICS guidance — establishment-level assignment

An enterprise can own multiple facilities with different principal activities. A Boeing-branded manufacturing plant may assemble airplanes while a Boeing-branded site may only perform aircraft maintenance. Similarly a FedEx-branded station can deliver parcels while another site sorts or holds goods. Industry assignment needs establishment-level operating evidence, not just parent-brand identity.

Training-only synthetic scenario; do not treat as a verified real-world establishment.
